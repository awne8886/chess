ChessLens

Scan a 2D chessboard on another monitor, tablet or laptop and see Stockfish's best move as live neon arrows. Everything runs on your phone.

  1. Tap ▶ and allow the rear camera.
  2. Frame the board in the upper half of the view.
  3. Drag the 4 corners onto the board's corners, then Lock grid. If the board shows the start position, it learns the piece set.
  4. Hold steady. When a move lands and the board stays still for 1 second, the position updates.
