ChessLens

Wave your phone over any 2D chessboard on a monitor, tablet or laptop. The grid locks on by itself, the pieces are read, and Stockfish's best move appears as a live neon arrow. Everything runs on your phone.

  1. Tap ▶ and allow the rear camera.
  2. Point it at the board. No calibration: the 8×8 lock turns neon green the moment the grid is found.
  3. The best move is drawn on the board in the camera view and on the mini-board. White / Black sets the side to move; Flip if the site shows the board from Black's side.
